2. Data collected when visiting this website

When you visit this website, our server automatically collects certain technical data transmitted by your browser: IP address, date and time of access, pages visited, browser type and version, operating system, and referrer URL. This data is processed on the basis of Art. 6 para. 1 lit. f GDPR (legitimate interest) to ensure the security and stability of our web infrastructure. Log files are deleted after 30 days.

Web analytics

We measure the reach of this website with Plausible Analytics, an open-source analytics software that we operate ourselves on our own server in Germany (stats.starflight-dynamics.com); no data is passed to the developer of the software or to any other third party. Plausible sets no cookies, stores no personal data and does not track you across websites; your IP address is not stored. We receive aggregated statistics only, such as page views, referring websites, country and device type, and use them to understand the reach of the website and improve it. Legal basis: Art. 6 (1)(f) GDPR; our legitimate interest is measuring the use of the website in a privacy-friendly way. You can object at any time (Art. 21 GDPR) by writing to hello@crestworks.co.

4. Newsletter

If you subscribe to our newsletter, we process your email address to send you updates. Legal basis: Art. 6 para. 1 lit. a GDPR (consent). You may unsubscribe at any time using the link in each newsletter.
